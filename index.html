How this prototype uses Cesar’s teaching

The lesson themes are inspired by publicly stated Cesar Millan concepts such as calm human energy, observation, timing, leadership, leash handling, trust, respect, and structured walking. The written instructions are original and use rewards, distance, repetition, management, and gradual exposure.

This is not Cesar Millan’s official course and is not affiliated with him. Videos remain optional and stream directly from his verified YouTube channel.

    Safety boundary

    Everyday puppy behaviors can be practiced with the training resources in this app. Stop the session and involve Frannie’s professional trainer when behavior becomes unsafe, frightening, or difficult for the person handling her to control.

    Frannie’s professional trainer’s specific instructions should take priority over general videos or guidance in this app. Use gates, closed doors, distance, leashes, and other management rather than deliberately provoking a difficult behavior. Serious aggression, severe fear, panic, or behavior that could cause injury should also be discussed with a veterinarian or qualified behavior professional when appropriate.

    Biting and nipping

    Normal puppy mouthing and overexcited nipping can be worked on with consistent training. End access to hands and clothing, redirect Frannie to an appropriate toy, reward calmer mouth behavior, and avoid rough hand play. The biting and nipping resources in the Training Video Library can be used for this kind of everyday puppy behavior.

    For hard bites, repeated snapping, guarding-related biting, bites intended to injure, or bites that break skin, stop self-guided practice and follow up with Frannie’s professional trainer or another qualified behavior professional.

    Stop the session when

    • Frannie becomes rigid, stares, snarls, snaps, or repeatedly lunges.
    • She cannot look away from the trigger or recover at the current distance.
    • She refuses food she normally values or appears overwhelmed.
    • The person handling Frannie feels frightened or cannot maintain control safely.
